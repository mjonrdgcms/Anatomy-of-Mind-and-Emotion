# The Seven Emotions: Heard and Screaming

The author's framing: the seven approaches are also seven emotions. Each emotion is a message from the heart, and each has a positive side, when the message is listened to, and a negative side, when it has to scream to get the message across. Each operates on one axis, and only one emotion can be in use at a time. The switching should be intuitive and fluid, and things get in the way.

This document establishes the two sides of each, so the interpreter can name which side a dream is showing. Sources: *Beyond Flat Land* ch. 13 (shadow forms), ch. 20 (the emotion-verb table and the sensory registers), ch. 28 (excess and deficiency); *World of Alphas* and *Postpartum* (the alarm that gets louder when overruled, the criterion, the courtroom, the two counterfeits, urgency as a counterfeit key).

## The rule behind all seven

The stress books give the mechanism that makes "heard" and "screaming" one rule rather than seven:

- **An alarm is a message with a volume knob.** Its content is a proposal (a verb). Its volume is a function of how long the proposal has gone unread. A message received early is quiet, specific, and cheap. The same message ignored, overruled, or argued with gets louder, because an overruled alarm is being told it is not informing but being suppressed, and it escalates.
- **Heard means: take the reading, do the verb, name a criterion.** The positive side of every emotion is the emotion delivering its verb at a low level with a checkable condition attached ("this is a forty; if he passes the pillar I move"). The message terminates when the criterion is met. The trigger list gets shorter over time.
- **Screaming means: the verb is applied without a criterion, at maximum, to the wrong object.** The negative side is always one of three things: the verb done to excess (the *Beyond Flat Land* excess pole), the verb refused until the body does it anyway (the deficiency pole, which is not quiet; it is the alarm running in the body while the mind claims restraint), or the verb aimed at a person instead of a thing (the shadow form of ch. 13, always in caricature).
- **The counterfeits are the screaming side's fuel.** Pleasure counterfeits joy and ranking counterfeits peace. Both read "the number went up, so this is the right direction." Every screaming form below runs on one of the two.
- **Only one at a time.** A screaming emotion holds the floor and blocks the switch. That is the main thing that "gets in the way": not the absence of the other six, but one of them refusing to yield the channel because its message has not been received. The positive side of each emotion therefore includes *finishing*, handing the channel back.

In dream terms: the heart runs all seven while the palette sleeps. A dream that shows one emotion's screaming form is the heart returning an unread message. The reading's job is to say which message, on which axis, and what criterion would let it stop.

## The seven in detail

### 1. Initiation. Contempt. Functionality.

**What the message says.** *Something is being presented for contact. Check whether it works before you take it in.* Contempt is the gate at the skin. Its axis is functionality: does this thing, person, idea, or offer do what it claims?

**Heard.** The gate opens enough to make a real appraisal, and then the decision is made with the thing actually in view. Declining is a valid outcome. Discernment is contempt received: the chill noticed, the source examined, and a verdict on the *function* rather than on the person. The interior test from the books: did the refusal follow contact, or precede it?

**Screaming.** Contempt that never gets its gate considered turns into repetitive dismissal: the person re-litigated in the shower, the idea rejected before its content arrives, the downward glance mistaken for evidence that the object is beneath consideration. The shadow form is charm turned on hard at the exact moment charm is an insult (the slick end). The deficiency end is inert or inaccessible: nothing is let in, so nothing can be appraised, and the skin does the recoil on its own. In the courtroom, this is the stacked jury: the verdict was in before the exhibit was examined.

**Dream signatures.** Gates, doors, hooks, skin and surfaces. Heard: a door opened and something examined at the threshold; a fish landed and looked at. Screaming: a door slammed before the visitor is seen; a wall of charm (a salesman, a mask, a coat) where a face should be; the skin crawling with no visible cause.

**Criterion that ends it.** Name what would show the thing functions. "If it does X, I take it; if not, I decline, and that is the end of it."

### 2. Deconstruction. Sadness. Reproducibility.

**What the message says.** *Something has been lost or has changed. Find its edge.* Sadness is the eye bringing a boundary into focus. Its axis is reproducibility: what exactly ended, what remains, and what can be made again, tested again, taken apart and rebuilt?

**Heard.** The loss is given an object. The one undifferentiated weight becomes several named losses, some of which can be reproduced and some of which cannot, and grief stays where it belongs instead of colouring every part of the life. Definition is not a solvent for grief; it is how grief stops being total.

**Screaming.** Two failures, one at each pole. Excess: analysis paralysis and the shadow form, "You're just doing X," the whole person reduced to one mechanism as though that settled something. Dismantling with no rebuilding. Deficiency: incurious, uncritical, the loss left with no edge, so that nothing finishes mattering ("people leave," "nothing I finish matters," which *Four Rivers* calls sand). In the stress books this is the hot memory retold with the charge intact: the content is known and the edge is never drawn, so it never gets filed.

**Dream signatures.** Blades, hammers, eyes, sand and stone. Heard: a thing cut open and examined; stone that can be built with. Screaming: a blade used on a person; endless dissection with nothing built; sand where a foundation should be; eyes that cannot focus, or a beam in the eye.

**Criterion that ends it.** "The thing I lost was Y. What I can still make is Z." When both halves are named, the eye can look away.

### 3. Expansion. Surprise. Perspective.

**What the message says.** *Something did not fit the category. Widen it.* Surprise is a prediction error in ordinary language, sampled fast at the tongue. Its axis is perspective: is this being seen at the right size, in the category that contains both what was expected and what happened?

**Heard.** The anomaly is kept long enough to widen the model, and no longer. The new category is the deliverable: not "that was odd" and not "everything is different now," but a better bucket. In the stress books this is "you always got more data": the reward prediction error treated as information, not as a threat.

**Screaming.** Excess: scatter, and the shadow form, kitchen-sinking. "And another thing," eleven grievances from four years pulled into one shapeless generalisation that cannot be answered because it is not a claim. Fascination with novelty for its own sake, which is gullibility. Deficiency: cramped and unimaginative, the anomaly filed as odd so the old category survives untouched. The counterfeit joy lives here: more stimulus, more new things, with nothing left behind afterward.

**Dream signatures.** Branches, birds, rainbows, taste and food, height for the view. Heard: a branch that reaches from a trunk; a taste followed by a decision. Screaming: branches with no trunk, sprawling; a flock of grievances with no single bird; a feast of things tasted and none swallowed; a view from so high nothing can be told apart.

**Criterion that ends it.** "The category that holds both is W." One sentence, one claim that could be wrong.

### 4. Unification. Happiness. Harmony.

**What the message says.** *This fits. Bring it in.* Happiness notices a fit worth preserving and proposes incorporate. Its axis is harmony: does this belong in the present whole, and will the whole still be viable with it in?

**Heard.** The good thing lands. The compliment is received, the achievement is acknowledged, the holiday is lived rather than documented. The household gets a table with everyone at it and no one forced. The two-people rule from the stress books applies: the aim is that both come out with something better than either had walking in, and a negotiation one person wins is a test the second line failed.

**Screaming.** Excess: intrusion, and the shadow form, weaponised togetherness. "After everything we've been to each other": the relationship itself produced as an instrument, the bond used as leverage. Harmony faked by conformity, intoxication, or a closed group that has excluded every difficult voice. Deficiency: isolating, fragmenting; the good thing deflected, never landed. The counterfeit peace lives here: the household keeping score, visible effort as currency, ranking within the marriage.

**Dream signatures.** Tables, yokes, cords, the herd and the flock, hearing and music. Heard: a table with four legs and people seated; a cord of three strands; a harp in tune. Screaming: everyone forced to the table; a yoke on one neck; a house with no table; a sound that fills the room and drowns every other voice; music out of tune.

**Criterion that ends it.** "This is in. Here is where it sits in the week." When the good thing has a place, happiness has done its job and can hand the channel over.

### 5. Implementation. Anger. Stability.

**What the message says.** *A real violation is happening. Hold.* Anger is pressure at the muscle and the chest, and it proposes hold: hold the boundary, hold the commitment, hold the tongue, hold to the evidence, hold the dignity. Its axis is stability: what must stay fixed so the rest can move?

**Heard.** The boundary is stated calmly and kept. Alternatives are given up on purpose. The referent is the actual violation, not whoever happened to trigger the pressure. The stress books' version: doing rather than proving; the wall of tested stones with its gates open.

**Screaming.** Excess: steamrolling, and the shadow form, "I've already done it," the unilateral decision announced as a fact. Force discharged at the trigger. The grip on the wrong thing tightened, which does not improve it. Deficiency: irresolute, ineffectual, the boundary never stated so the body holds it instead (the arm that will not yield, the shoulders funded for a fight that is not happening). This is the Too Strong pattern: suspended, holding everything, proving nothing can move it, at a cost disproportionate to the benefit. Its defended verdict is *not weak*.

**Dream signatures.** Rods, staffs, ploughs, muscle, the chest, the ox. Heard: a rod that steadies; a plough that goes in a line; a grip that holds one thing. Screaming: the rod swung as a club; the chest that cannot breathe out; a beast dragging the plough over people; a wall being built while someone is still inside.

**Criterion that ends it.** "The line is here. If it is crossed, I do this. Otherwise I am done holding." Anger with a criterion becomes a boundary; anger without one becomes a posture.

### 6. Preservation. Fear. Certainty.

**What the message says.** *Something is exposed. Secure it.* Fear is felt in the stomach and the breath before it is verbal, and it proposes take: take cover, take the precaution, take the appointment, take the chance while it exists. Its axis is certainty: how much needs to be known, stored, or insured against before exposure becomes loss?

**Heard.** The precaution is taken, once, with a criterion. The alarm is graded (this is a forty), the risk is assessed rather than the fear treated as the assessment, and the check terminates. The stress books' three conditions for verification: checkable, correctable, terminates. Manna dosed daily; the storehouse with an inventory.

**Screaming.** Excess: control, and the shadow form, the freeze-out. Not an argument at all; the wall goes up, the account is closed, the ground is salted so nothing can grow there later. The wall with no gate. Checking that never terminates, because no criterion was set, so nothing can ever count as enough certainty. Manna hoarded breeds worms. Deficiency: careless, unprepared, the fear overruled instead of read, which is exactly the move that makes it louder. This is the Too Nice pattern (matching, yielding, never taking the precaution) and the Too Self-Sufficient pattern (taking everything into the self so nothing has to be trusted). Its defended verdict is *wrong to have trusted*. The counterfeit peace runs here too: safety as ranking, certainty as "more."

**Dream signatures.** Walls, shells, storehouses, the stomach and lungs, insects and the turtle. Heard: a wall with a gate; a store with a known amount in it; a full breath; a guardian at the threshold letting the right thing through. Screaming: the wall without a gate; a swarm guarding what does not need guarding; a store that rots; breath held; ice on the well.

**Criterion that ends it.** "The exposure is E. The precaution is P. Once P is done, I re-check at time T and not before."

### 7. Transformation. Disgust. Excellence.

**What the message says.** *Something no longer fits where it is. Let it go and realign.* Disgust arrives at a distance, before inspection, and it proposes release. Its axis is excellence: do the process and the result still belong together, and what must be put down for them to match again?

**Heard.** The misaligned thing is released: a method, an aim, an identity, a claim to control, a once-useful arrangement that has reached its limit. The comparison is between process and result, not between the self and other people. The breath goes out so that it can come in. Kintsugi: the break chooses the spot, the mending chooses the material. Enough distance to see whether the structure and its result still belong together, without contempt for the structure that carried you here.

**Screaming.** Excess: grandiose, and the shadow form, "This proves the whole relationship was a mistake," the sudden zoom-out that erases the actual complaint. Moral disgust that makes exclusion feel like perception and converts a person into contamination. Living five years ahead of the ground. Deficiency: shortsighted, stagnant, nothing ever put down; the boulder carried up the mountain because it is too big to surrender. The counterfeit here is purity as ranking: cleaner than, better than, above.

**Dream signatures.** Summits, fixed lights, the sabbath, the breath, fire from heaven, amphibians and the eagle. Heard: a boulder let go at the summit; fire that consumes the costume and leaves the real thing; a breath released. Screaming: living on the crest and never coming down; fire aimed at a person; the whole house condemned for one spoiled room; a breath that will not go out.

**Criterion that ends it.** "What I am putting down is Q. What the result should look like once it is down is R." If the letting-go is aimed at a person, it is not Transformation; it is the courtroom.

## How the interpreter uses this

1. **Find the loudest emotion in the dream.** Not the one the dreamer felt on waking (that is data too, and goes in the intake), but the one the dream's action is running: what is being received, cut, widened, joined, held, stored, or released, and at what volume.
2. **Say which side it is on.** The screaming forms above have recognisable shapes: the object of the verb is a person; the verb is done at maximum; no criterion is in sight; the axis is being used as a verdict.
3. **Name the unread message.** A screaming emotion in a dream is the heart returning a proposal that has gone unread in waking life. The reading names the proposal in its quiet form: not "you are afraid" but "something needs one precaution taken and then left alone."
4. **Say what is blocking the switch.** Only one emotion holds the channel at a time. If the dream shows one screaming, ask what it would need in order to finish and hand over. Usually the answer is a criterion.
5. **End with the criterion.** The one thing to check, stated so that it can be met and the alarm can stand down. This is the same guard as the rest of the method: the dream proposes, waking life disposes.

## Points for the author to confirm

1. **Is the deficiency pole "screaming" too?** I have read it that way: refusing the verb does not silence the message, it moves the alarm into the body (the arm test, the held breath, the shoulders). So both excess and deficiency are the negative side, and the only positive side is the verb done at the right dose with a criterion. If you intend the negative side to be only the loud, excess, shadow form, the table needs trimming.
2. **The axis words.** I have treated each axis as the question the emotion is asking (does it function, can it be reproduced, is it in proportion, does it harmonise, does it stand, is it certain enough, is it excellent) and the screaming form as that question turned into a verdict on a person. If the axes are meant more as the *domain* the emotion governs rather than its question, some of the wording above should change.
3. **Counterfeit placement.** I have put counterfeit joy (pleasure) under Expansion and counterfeit peace (ranking) under Unification and Preservation, because that is where the books' examples fall. They may belong to all seven equally.
4. **The switch.** "Things get in the way" I have read as: a screaming emotion holds the channel. There may be other blockers you have in mind (the 17% filter, the palette's two and a half, a taboo on one approach), and those could be listed as a second class of obstruction.

# The Theory, As I Understand It

This is a restatement of the system in *The Four Rivers of Paradise* and *Beyond Flat Land*, written so the author can check whether an interpreter built on it would be reading dreams the way the books read them. Where I am unsure, I say so. Page and chapter references point at the manuscripts.

## 1. The two countries

Everything rests on one division: **the heart and the mind do not share a country.**

| | The heart (earth) | The mind (sky) |
|---|---|---|
| Work | Love: recognises value before qualification, hopes the good, receives life and lets it pass outward | Logic: separates one thing from another, holds both where they can be compared, finds the rule |
| Nature of its contents | Immeasurable, infinite, not under control. Nobody decides to love or stop. | Measurable, finite, bounded. A definition is only useful because it has edges. |
| Position | Low | High |
| Light | Dark, invisible, black | Bright, visible, white |
| Shape | Round (encloses by continuing) | Square (encloses by distinction, four judgments) |
| Substance | Water, soft, flowing | Hard, rock, structure |
| Animal family | Mammal (warm interior, feeds young from its body) | Reptile (hard articulated surface, heat from the light above) |
| Metal | Gold (buried, warm, does not tarnish, conducts) | Silver (bright, reflective, the mirror and the coin) |
| Gender | Feminine, mother earth | Masculine, sky father |
| Hand | Left | Right |
| Compass needle | Joy (love on course) | Peace (logic on course) |
| Counterfeit needle | Pleasure (the feeling without a course) | Safety (stillness of a locked room) |
| Opposite pole | Vindictiveness (wanting it twisted, at any cost) | Hostility (wanting it gone, at any cost) |
| Instrument | Wisdom | Knowledge |

These are described as "one word spoken in twelve forms," not twelve comparisons. Some pairs carry more than others: black/white is weak (two positions on one scale); water/light carries most, because they differ in kind. Water gives life and light keeps the water pure, so the vessel is walled at the sides and **open at the top**.

The heart comes first, the mind gives it banks. Neither is ours: feelings arrive and thoughts pass, and no discipline makes them ask permission. What is ours is the **will**, the trunk between the two.

## 2. What a dream is and what it is for

- The ordinary dream is made only of what is already in the dreamer. Nothing arrives from outside. The dreamer is "the only paint."
- The mind is off duty. Whatever assembles the dream does not care about architecture, chronology, or the fact that the house was sold; it cares about **what things mean**, and will violate every other rule to get that right. That assembler is the heart.
- So the dream is the heart's picture of the meaningful shape of the situation the dreamer is already in. Not an instruction, not a prediction.
- **The nightly job is sorting.** The day carries things in mixed together. The dream separates them: heart things into the heart's vessel (bottomless, because meaning has no bottom) and mind things into the mind's vessel (finite, because a definition needs edges). A heart thing handled with the mind's instruments is dissected instead of received. A mind thing dropped into the heart's water sinks without being measured. This is the "wrong box" problem, and *Beyond Flat Land* names it as the first junction nearest the source: meaningfulness vs. logic. The dream works at that junction.
- Psyche's heap of mixed seed, sorted overnight by ants (or mice, or doves) beyond the strength of the conscious soul, is the image of this. Guardians of the heart, on sorting duty, for free.
- The Islamic three-way sort applies: a true dream (from God, about outside events, e.g. Joseph's stars), a disturbing dream, and the dream from the self. **The grammar is for the third kind, which is nearly every dream.**
- Dreams often come in **threes**: the first lays down the general situation (subject, verb, object); the second returns to the part that matters and modifies it (adjectives, adverbs, pressures); the third drills into that one element. Context, emphasis, depth. A dream doubled is not repeated because it failed; it is being established (Pharaoh's cows and ears).

## 3. The two-symbol rule

The dictionary does not change with the interpreter. A round silver object is a compound statement, not a contradiction. The work is never to decide what a word means; it is to **read the sentence the words make together**: water on a mountain, a tree in a kitchen, a stairway ending in the earth.

The tell that the dream register is at play, in a dream or in a story or a painting: **the same country said twice in one image.** A she-bear is mammal plus mother; a fish with a gold coin in its mouth is the heart's deep plus the heart's metal. When two or more markers from one country stack, read it in the grammar.

Each reading is a **slice**: true about what passed through it, incomplete about the whole. Four rivers, four positions, four loves, four corruptions, four paths, four colours are projections through one object from different angles; the structure repeats because it belongs to the object, not the image.

## 4. The self

**The tree is the self, specifically the will.** Roots in the dark soil of the heart, drinking water nobody can see. Branches up in the light, mapping the physical space of reality, where fruit can be counted. Trunk between, which is the only part that is ours: we can reach every part of both countries through it and can be neither. A tree is the shape of a choice. Fruit is what the choosing yields. Cut off the root and the crown is wood. Cut off the crown and the root has nowhere to send what it drinks.

- **Idol**: a tree cut down, roots severed, carved into a shape that will never change, set upright in a room. Outline of a living thing with no traffic. Diagnostic question: is anything moving through this?
- **Staff / rod**: a choice cut to carrying size, a decision a man can put weight on and walk with. It can be laid down (Zechariah cuts Beauty and Bands) or laid up (Aaron's rod buds).
- **Cross**: the other carved tree, cut and squared, with everything moving through it.
- **Fig leaves**: the mask. A leaf advertises sweetness; it is not the fruit. Stripping leaves for masks blinds the tree to the light it makes sweetness from.
- **Two counterfeit trees**: the tree of knowledge (all is mind, crown passed off as the plant) and the invisible tree (all is heart, roots with no crown and no trunk). They twist together into an arch that photographs like a trunk and connects nothing.

**The house is the dreamer.** A self as enclosure. Rooms are compartments (what is permitted in one room is not permitted in another). A doorway lets traffic pass between parts; a window lets one part see another without passing; a partition with no door is a part sealed off; a door that will not shut is a part that can keep nothing out. **A roof is a mistake** (the paralytic lowered through the opened roof): sealed overhead, nothing comes down and the water goes foul.

- **Kitchen**: where raw experience is prepared into something the person can take in. **Food is raw experience.** Ten thousand unprepared events do not equal one opened, divided, understood, incorporated.
- **Bathroom**: releases what must not remain. Bathroom distress in dreams (clogged, doorless, filthy, no privacy) is experience that could not be assimilated and has no orderly way out, now spreading beyond the room meant to contain it. Not a character failure; waste is produced by successful nourishment too.
- **Table**: a hard plane on which experience is separated and prepared. The altar is a table. **The surface of the table is attention; the four legs are the four loves.** Leg thickness is load-bearing capacity (temperament, fine to be unequal); leg height is extension (must be equal or the table tilts, and the quiet, low-adhesion things slide off, leaving only the sticky ones: fear, desire, resentment, shame, emergency).
- **Mouth**: a two-way gate, food in and words out. **Teeth are memories and testimonies**, at the mind's level core assumptions ("people leave," "caution keeps me alive"). A tooth breaking or falling out is an assumption meeting something harder than itself. The person is not breaking; an instrument is. Milk teeth were only the first set built to fall out; the shark grows rows.

**The ark is the completed self-concept**, packed for travel: tables of stone (the mind; the law broken and rewritten, the broken first set kept too), the golden pot of manna (the heart; the question "what is this?" kept fresh in an unreactive vessel), Aaron's budded rod (the will laid before the presence). Built with carrying poles never removed. It did not survive an address.

**The cup / grail** is the same anatomy: bowl (heart), stem (will), foot (mind). A heart of stone is not a broken vessel; it is a sound vessel filled with its own record, with no room left. The promise is not stronger material; it is the room.

**The candle**: wax is the heart's work (made of tallow and beeswax, the rivers' own substances), the candlestick is the mind's framework, the flame is truth from above. Wax without flame is a candle-shaped object. A bushel does not unlight the candle; it smothers it. Fixed lights (sun, moon, stars) rule and set; the candle is carried, lamp-to-the-feet, into rooms and around to the angle where the obvious becomes visible.

## 5. People, children, animals, vehicles

**Other people in a dream are parts of the dreamer.** Personification is a delivery mechanism: some things cannot be told to a man directly and have to be walked in wearing a face. The practical key: **ask for three words that describe this person.** One, often all three, names the organ of the dreamer's own psyche wearing that face. (Guard from *Beyond Flat Land*: a dream figure can also carry learned features of the real person; the useful question is whether the figure has the person's actual range or has been compressed to one function, and what the compression made possible.)

**Babies and children are integration products**: what is born when the heart and the mind come together and make a new part of the dreamer, more integrated with logic and love than either parent faculty alone. A realisation, a reconciliation, a capacity that did not exist before, young and needing to be fed. A dream with a child in it is usually about how the newborn thing is being cared for. Bound instead of raised (Loki's brood), it grows against the chain. The newest, most integrated part is the one that draws the sword lightly.

**Animals are relationships**: the one thing in a man's world with its own will, that can be fed or starved, comes when called or does not, can turn. **The species is the adverb**: it names the facet through which the dreamer and that person chiefly interface.

| Animal | Reading |
|---|---|
| Dog | Loyalty (nose that follows what nothing visible announces; jaw that holds on) |
| Horse | Motivation (carries you forward when your legs would quit) |
| Cat | Grace and independence (comes and goes on its own terms) |
| Cow | Giving (the Norse cow with four rivers of milk fed a giant and a stranger, then vanished) |
| Sheep / flock | Comfort; a man's kept relationships |
| Bear, she-bear | The heart's animal in its most absolute form: the mother, for whom the exposed thing is a charge, not a target |
| Lion | Courage (the thing it costs to break the stone table head on) |
| Wolf | Autonomy, the animal that will not join the household; bound only by impossibilities |
| Fish | Something alive in the heart's deep, brought up on a hook cast into water we cannot see into |
| Bee | Obedience to a given aim; makes the third river |
| Turtle | The mind's family, the walking enclosure; the shell is the refused fallacies |
| Eagle | Wings that are alive and given (being carried up, not climbing scaffolding) |
| Crow | What gets behind you at the worst hour and is not a person |
| **Serpent** | **Attention.** The one cold animal, the mind's animal, the one thing in the mind that behaves like an animal. Goes down holes and up trees, passes any gap wider than its head, shaped by what it swallows. A floor of snakes: everything demanding attention at once. A snake in the grass: a claim not yet seen, already near the ankle. A bite: attention seized, the body organised around one puncture. Three verbs: sink it (it grows to fit the sea and encircles the world), lift it (the pole, the healing), wing it (Quetzalcoatl). Scales are pride's armour; they fall from the eyes first. |
| **Insects** | **Guardians of the heart**, the soul's white blood cells: mindless and protective, which can be good or bad. They defend whatever they are posted on, a real breach or a husk. A dream crawling with them is a guard response, and the question is what they are guarding and whether it should be guarded. Ants sort Psyche's seed. |

**Vehicles are how a man moves through the world of men.** Built, no will, only capacity and route. Read any vehicle by two measurements: **how many it holds** and **how much it runs on rails.** Train (fixture of society, huge capacity, total rigidity), bus/caravan (routes others set), car (the "us"-sized vehicle), truck (movement tied to a task), motorcycle (essentially alone), bicycle (alone, and honest that the man is the engine), shoes (the smallest vehicle; barefoot is no vehicle at all), chariot (the category itself; trusting it is the temptation). A **boat** is the built structure a relationship floats on over the heart's water; when it founders, only a living thing carries (Jonah's fish, the hand for Peter). **Flying** divides: built flight (Babel's masonry, Icarus's wax, the airplane as a train of the sky) is intellectualisation, moving to the upper country to avoid the ground floor; given flight (eagle's wings) is being carried. An idol is a vehicle in reverse: it must be carried.

## 6. Water, stone, fire, metal

**Water is the heart's element**, love as it moves. Still water is a mirror and gives you your face (Narcissus stops at the surface; the adjusted mirror is silt that softens the parts a man cannot look at). Moving water takes you somewhere. Wells are where the betrothals happen. The sea is the heart's deep; what must be recovered is down and under water and reached by descent. **Flood** is boundaries failing (fountains below and windows above at once). **Marsh** is water with no banks, and marshes are not healed. **Ice** is water arrested by fear; the frozen well is the coldest corruption, and the cure is not repair or effort but heat: be still, stop flapping. Toxicity is never an ingredient; it is arrested motion (stopped, sealed, bent back, or substituted).

**Stone is memory**, testimony, "set in the shape it was in when it set." Heart-knowledge made firm enough for the mind to build with. Jacob's pillow stood upright is a memory put on the altar. **Sand** is stones too small to build with: worn-down testimony, conclusions with the witnesses gone ("people leave," "nothing I finish matters"). Sand cannot be a foundation but can be a boundary. **Desert** is the country of sand and no trees: no live choices; where the new creature wanders after baptism; forty. **Library** is the opposite of desert: memories that have been read, the past legible and larger than the time given to it. **Boulder** is testimony grown to the size where it must be enthroned or surrendered. **Lava** is stone unfixed, memory melted back to liquid by heat. **Glass** is fused sand, the past made into the one surface light passes through; the harp on the sea of glass is tuning, the tension right.

**Wall / enclosure** is made of thinking that has been tested and found to hold. Paradise means the walled place; *jannah* means the covered place. The wall exists to make an inside, so the water has banks and a direction; its gates are never shut. **The altar** is the mind's stable map of a living reality, a stone plane with four horns, on which a living thing (a relationship, a work, a memory) is bound in every direction at once so it cannot circle its post, opened with the blade (logic), and tested by fire. What the altar keeps is never the bond; it is whatever the bond was mistaken for. **Idol vs altar**: the idol is a plane mistaken for the whole; the altar is a plane that knows it is a plane.

**Fire from heaven is truth**, the audit that consumes what is costume and cannot find a thread on what is real. It descends only the distance the man could not close by climbing. Light keeps the water pure; the vessel is open at the top for it. Sun is truth; moon is love (reflecting a light it does not generate); stars are the noble examples.

**Metals**: gold is the finished heart; lead is the same substance arrested (resentment: small doses, faintly sweet, stored in the bones); silver is the mind's coin and mirror; iron is stone with metal asleep in it. **The sword is truth made from memory by fire and hammering**; a broken sword's shards are kept because the stronger blade is in them. **Diamond** is carbon bonded in all four directions (the four loves), with no sliding plane, carried up through fire. **Pearl** is worth layered around a grain of sand, the heart's way with memory. The alchemical colours run black, white, yellow, red, which is water, milk, honey, wine.

### Adding and subtracting

The heart only adds, and the mind only subtracts. Love gives; logic cuts (the blade, the test, the boundary). Each is right in its own country. The two opposite poles of *Four Rivers* are what each does when it stops counting the cost. The mind, not caring about consequences, subtracts: it is **hostile**, and wants something gone. The heart, not caring about consequences, adds: it is **vindictive**, and wants something put into the other person. The commonest thing vindictiveness wants to add is **regret**, so that the other person changes. The two look different and have the same defect, an uncounted cost, so the same questions answer both (`docs/context-and-definitions.md`, section 5).

## 7. The four rivers

Four is the count a body gives: front, back, two sides. There are four ways two people can stand and no fifth.

| River | Position | Love | Colour | Path (India) | Great (Tao) | Corruptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water | Face to face | Eros. The other is the entire field of view. The rival stands here too. | Black | Stilled attention | The Tao | Flood (no banks) / stagnation (no motion). The mirror. |
| Milk | Back to back | Storge. Given before it can be asked; designed to be outgrown; weaning is the feast. | White | Devotion | Earth | Refusing to rotate (the witch's gingerbread house) / turning the gift into a debt ("after all I have done") |
| Honey | Shoulder to shoulder | Philia. Two facing a third thing; must be made; does not spoil; kills insecurity by osmosis. | Yellow | Action | The king | Devotion to a corrupt object (the multiplier has no sign) / the circle turning inward |
| Wine | One behind the other | Agape. The only asymmetric one. Antiseptic and anaesthetic. Pours forward and does not drink. | Red | Knowledge (discrimination of the two burdens) | Heaven | Abandonment (withholding the anaesthetic) / possession (taking the man's own pack) |

Two rivers stay in the garden (face to face, back to back: the household); two go out and get names (shoulder to shoulder, behind: the world). A home with only the inner two goes stale; a man with only the outer two has nowhere to sleep.

**Alone, each love circles its post**: charity alone becomes enabling, friendship alone conspiracy, affection alone tyranny, the first position alone seals the room. In every case the failing love is doing *more* of itself. **Your strongest love is where you will go wrong**, and the love that corrects it is the one you are worst at. The four rivers rise from the buried end of the tree, never down from the branches.

**Worship** is a body in a position: water says *you are worth being taken apart by*; milk *worth letting go of*; honey *worth standing beside*; wine *worth carrying*. Love is unconditional (left hand); trust is conditional and has an object (right hand). Marriage is built on trust.

## 8. Recurring machinery

- **Serpent on the pole**: attention on the ground is the wound; attention lifted is the cure; same animal.
- **The leprechaun / husk (klipah)**: the small drowner. What pulls a man under is small bodies, in numbers, while he sleeps. Caught and held (by attention), it grants the breath. One a day. The daily catch is cheaper than the sea monster. You cannot catch someone else's.
- **The triangle**: the first move when something goes wrong is to recruit a third party as a jury for where the shame should sit. The altar is the alternative to the game.
- **The mirror**: the adjusted one (silt) vs. the unadjusted (the enemy, the mistreated servant, water unaltered).
- **Manna**: daily uncertainty, "what is this?", dosed. Hoarded, it breeds worms. Bread is the first job; wine (lifting hands) is the second; both are entirely inside the will.
- **The mercy seat**: where a man goes when he tries to love as he was loved; the surgery you learn by assisting at another's; "notice what pulls you out, and go back in."
- **Circling vs. the wheel**: one attachment makes an orbit; four stations make a spiral. Indistinguishable from inside; the test is whether the returning landmark sits higher.
- **Sabbath / carnival**: the licensed circle, inversion held open on purpose, holy because it ends.
- **Kintsugi**: the break chooses the spot; the mending chooses the material.
- **"Let it die"**: the altar measures the length of the rope. Two guards: nobody consecrates what he hoped to lose; Cain killed to solve his own problem.

## 9. The Beyond Flat Land layer

The second book supplies the mind's side of the sorting, one junction downstream.

- Seven approaches (bridges): Initiation, Deconstruction, Expansion, Unification, Implementation, Preservation, Transformation. People run about two and a half under load (ego, persona, shadow), and the shadow appears in caricature.
- Seven emotions as messages carrying a verb: contempt → receive (Initiation); sadness → isolate/define (Deconstruction); surprise → expand (Expansion); happiness → incorporate (Unification); anger → hold (Implementation); fear → take (Preservation); disgust → let go (Transformation). Reception, not obedience. The author's axes for the seven (functionality, reproducibility, perspective, harmony, stability, certainty, excellence) and the heard and screaming side of each are in `docs/seven-emotions.md`.
- Two questions, not two verdicts: **joy** (does this matter?) and **peace** (does this hold together?). Joy without peace: the story keeps changing and the certainty does not. Peace without joy: everything coheres and no one can say what it is for.
- Two routing errors, with predicted dream signatures: **proxy capture** appears as the furniture of calculation imposed on what matters (weighed, counted, gridded, in rows); **meaning as amnesty** appears as the ground failing to hold (water where the floor should be, a house adrift).
- Function-first rule: begin with what a thing does in relation to a body, then check against this dream and this life.
- Guards the interpreter must keep: the dream proposes, waking life disposes; a dream is a hypothesis about your model of the world, never evidence about another person; a reading should account for several features, connect to something specific in waking life, not ignore the elements that push the other way, and end in a question, observation, or action that can be checked outside the dream; resonance is weak evidence; interpretation is an accelerant, not a requirement; if it increases certainty faster than contact with reality, set it down.

## 9b. The stress-physiology layer (*World of Alphas*, *Postpartum*)

The two stress books supply the body's side: what happens when a message from the heart goes unread.

- **Alpha mode** is a cost disproportionate to the benefit: the body funded for a fight that is not happening. The lion is not there. The 17% filter: most of the alarm's triggers are for lions that are not there, and the practice is to shorten the trigger list, not to quiet the alarm.
- **An overruled alarm gets louder.** Suppressing or arguing with a signal tells the system it is being overruled rather than informed. Disarming has two parts: release the muscles that were funded for the fight (the breath and the shoulders, the physiological sigh, five-second yoga), then listen to what the alarm says and challenge the assumptions it is made of.
- **Levels and criteria.** A threat response drops from maximum when a named, observable criterion is attached: "this is a forty; if he passes the pillar, I move; re-check at seven." A reading, not a verdict. "This is only a forty so stop it" is a debate, and a debate is itself a threat signal.
- **Courtroom vs boardroom.** The courtroom runs on verdict, a stacked jury, tampered evidence, and proving rather than doing; its fuel is fear and greed as urgency. The boardroom asks four questions and runs on maybe (the farmer's maybe). Recruiting a jury for where the shame should sit is the triangle in *Four Rivers*.
- **The arm test** shows three defended patterns and their verdicts: Too Nice (matching; *not selfish*), Too Strong (suspended; *not weak*), Too Self-Sufficient (yield and reclaim; *wrong to have trusted*). Each is a message from the heart held in the muscle instead of read.
- **Two compasses, two counterfeits.** Joy (does this matter?) is counterfeited by pleasure, which leaves the appetite rather than something behind. Peace (does this hold together?) is counterfeited by coming out ahead of somebody, a position that must be re-established every time it is checked. Both counterfeits read "the number went up."
- **Urgency as a counterfeit key.** If nothing gets done unless it is on fire, the nervous system learns to light fires. The most meaningful fifth of a life never screams and loses every scheduling contest; it has to be given a Thursday at seven.
- **The REM editing room.** Sleep keeps a memory's content and strips its charge, when noradrenaline is low enough to let it. A hot memory retold with the charge intact never gets filed. This is the physiological side of "dreams sort what got filed in the wrong box."
- **What the dreams are sorting** (*Postpartum* ch. 26). The commonest mis-sort is a person or a relationship filed under evidence: held with the mind's tools (proof, verdict, ledger) when it belongs to the heart. A recurring dream is the same grain returned until it is filed. The waking question is: what have I been holding with the wrong tools?
- **Love unconditional, trust conditional.** There is no control in love. The wall is logic and trust; the garden is love (squeezing the puppy). The deepest motivation is to hope the best and create synergy.
- **Verification** must be checkable, correctable, and terminate. A check with no termination is the wall with no gate.

## 10. Where I would like confirmation

1. **Scope.** Should the interpreter read with the Four Rivers grammar alone, or also run the Beyond Flat Land layer (which approach the heart is suggesting, which emotion-verb, joy/peace)? My reading is that they are two junctions of one pipe and both belong, with the Four Rivers reading primary.
2. **Sorting as the headline.** You said the main goal of dreams is to separate what got sorted in the wrong box. I read that as: every interpretation should end by naming the misfiled thing, i.e. the heart-thing being measured or the mind-thing being drowned. Is that the right emphasis, above the four-rivers reading?
3. **The three-words rule.** Only the dreamer can supply the three words for a person. I would build the interpreter to ask rather than guess. Same for animals: it should ask who the animal is, then read the species.
4. **Scripture in the output.** The books read scripture and dreams in one language. Should the interpreter cite the parallels by default, or keep readings in plain terms with the parallels available on request?
5. **"Black" and "rock."** You listed black and rock for the two countries. The book uses dark and hard (and treats stone as memory specifically). I have kept both, with stone flagged as memory.

# The Seven Value Cards

Playing-card size (63 × 88 mm), one card per approach, front and back. The front is for recognising the approach; the back is for using it. Text is written to fit at 6 to 7 point; every line below is meant to be printed as is.

## Layout shared by all seven

**Front**
- Top band in the approach's colour: the number and name on the left, the **value word** on the right (what this approach measures).
- Below the band, the **drawing** (two thirds of the card).
- Under the drawing, three short lines: *Emotion → verb*, *Body*, *Question*.
- Bottom strip: a tiny heptagon locator with this card's side filled in its colour and the opposite vertex marked, with the names of the two opposite approaches beside it.

**Back**
- Top band repeats number, name and the **bridge**: the dichotomy this approach crosses, written as *asset pole ↔ risk pole*.
- A 2 × 2 grid of the four **tools**, each with its symbol pair and its **weapon** in smaller type underneath. Columns: Concrete (square family) and Abstract (round family). Rows: Asset (filled) and Risk (hollow).
- Two lines: *Heard* and *Screaming*.
- One line: *Too much / too little*.
- Bottom line, in italics: the question to ask about someone who is good at this.

The data cube key (printed once on a separate card or the box): ■ ● raw asset, ┘ ◗ interpreted asset, ┌ ◐ interpreted risk, □ ○ raw risk. Filled is asset, hollow is risk; square is concrete, round is abstract; a piece missing means interpreted.

## 1. Initiation — Functionality (orange)

**Front**
- Band: **1 · Initiation** · *Functionality*
- Emotion → verb: **Contempt → receive, or decline**
- Body: **the skin**
- Question: **Does it work?**
- Opposites: Expansion · Transformation

**Drawing.** A bare hand at the threshold of a half-open door, fingertips just touching the wood. On the far side of the door, light. A small fish-hook hangs from the door handle on a line. The skin of the hand is the focus: drawn with visible texture, the only part of the image rendered in detail. The door frame is square and plain. Nothing else in the room.

**Back**
- Band: **Initiation** · *Interactive ↔ Speculative*
- Tools:
  - Concrete asset ┘■ **Confidence**: decisiveness, options. Weapon: *Competitive* (impatience, indecision)
  - Abstract asset ◗● **Enterprise**: autonomy, anticipation. Weapon: *Exploitation* (audacity, conjecture)
  - Concrete risk ┌□ **Functionality**: emulation, recognition. Weapon: *Intimidation* (unoriginality, jealousy)
  - Abstract risk ◐○ **Cleverness**: strategy, inference. Weapon: *Cheating* (indiscriminate, scapegoating)
- Heard: contact first, then a real appraisal, then engage or decline.
- Screaming: the sneer; the idea rejected before it arrives; charm turned on as an insult.
- Too much / too little: slick, shallow / inert, inaccessible.
- *Who do you know who makes contact with a problem before judging it? What would they do here?*

## 2. Deconstruction — Reproducibility (blue)

**Front**
- Band: **2 · Deconstruction** · *Reproducibility*
- Emotion → verb: **Sadness → isolate and define**
- Body: **the eye**
- Question: **What exactly is this, and can it be made again?**
- Opposites: Transformation · Unification

**Drawing.** A single open eye in profile, and in front of it a blade resting on a stone that has been split cleanly in two. The two halves are drawn so that the grain inside matches, proving the cut was true. A single small scale, like a fish scale, is falling from the lower lid. Everything in sharp line, no shading except inside the stone.

**Back**
- Band: **Deconstruction** · *Simple ↔ Complex*
- Tools:
  - Concrete asset ┘■ **Objectivity**: accuracy, concentration. Weapon: *Self-fulfilling* (selectivity, rumination)
  - Abstract asset ◗● **Subjectivity**: context, immersion. Weapon: *Misuse* (stereotype, indulgence)
  - Concrete risk ┌□ **Empiricism**: categorization, corroboration. Weapon: *Intolerance* (hypercritical, skeptical)
  - Abstract risk ◐○ **Causation**: insight, antithesis. Weapon: *Instigation* (superstition, rebelliousness)
- Heard: the loss given an edge; what ended, what remains, what can be remade.
- Screaming: "you're just doing X"; analysis with nothing rebuilt; a loss with no edge that colours everything.
- Too much / too little: paralysed by analysis / incurious, uncritical.
- *Who do you know who can take a thing apart and say exactly what is there? What would they do here?*

---

## 3. Expansion — Perspective (green)

**Front**
- Band: **3 · Expansion** · *Perspective*
- Emotion → verb: **Surprise → expand toward it**
- Body: **the tongue**
- Question: **What larger category holds both what I expected and what happened?**
- Opposites: Implementation · Initiation

**Drawing.** A tree seen from below, the trunk at the bottom edge, branches spreading to fill the top of the card. On one branch sits a small bird, and the bird is tasting a berry: tongue visible, head tilted. Behind the branches a faint arc of a rainbow. The branches are many but all visibly join the one trunk.

**Back**
- Band: **Expansion** · *Specific ↔ General*
- Tools:
  - Concrete asset ┘■ **Innovation**: pioneering, openness. Weapon: *Entertainment* (ostentatious, paradox)
  - Abstract asset ◗● **Imagination**: vision, creativity. Weapon: *Helplessness* (wishfulness, distractibility)
  - Concrete risk ┌□ **Humility**: learning, patience. Weapon: *Laziness* (skimming, procrastination)
  - Abstract risk ◐○ **Holism**: implications, consideration. Weapon: *Eccentric* (desperate, childishness)
- Heard: the anomaly kept just long enough to find the better category.
- Screaming: "and another thing": eleven grievances fused into one non-claim; novelty for its own sake.
- Too much / too little: scattered / cramped, unimaginative.
- *Who do you know who can see the bigger picture without losing the case in front of them? What would they do here?*

---

## 4. Unification — Harmony (gold)

**Front**
- Band: **4 · Unification** · *Harmony*
- Emotion → verb: **Happiness → incorporate**
- Body: **the ear**
- Question: **Does this belong in the whole, and does the whole still work with it in?**
- Opposites: Deconstruction · Preservation

**Drawing.** A round table with four legs, seen slightly from above, with four unmatched chairs around it, none forced in and one still being pulled up. Above the table a single ear, large and open, drawn as if the table were listening. On the table, a cord of three strands, loosely coiled. Warm light.

**Back**
- Band: **Unification** · *Future ↔ Present*
- Tools:
  - Concrete asset ┘■ **Receptivity**: amenability, responsiveness. Weapon: *Erraticness* (permissiveness, fakeness)
  - Abstract asset ◗● **Hope**: opportunity, optimism. Weapon: *Passive-aggressive* (dramatic, prattling)
  - Concrete risk ┌□ **Continuity**: maintenance, follow-up. Weapon: *Tyranny* (invasiveness, nosey)
  - Abstract risk ◐○ **Synergy**: tact, compatibility. Weapon: *Enmeshment* (self-effacing, hypersensitivity)
- Heard: the good thing lands and gets a place in the week.
- Screaming: "after everything we've been to each other"; harmony by conformity or a closed room.
- Too much / too little: intrusive / isolating, fragmenting.
- *Who do you know who brings people together without forcing anyone? What would they do here?*

## 5. Implementation — Stability (red)

**Front**
- Band: **5 · Implementation** · *Stability*
- Emotion → verb: **Anger → hold**
- Body: **the chest, the muscle**
- Question: **What must stay fixed so the rest can move?**
- Opposites: Preservation · Expansion

**Drawing.** A forearm and hand gripping a wooden rod that is planted upright in the ground, the ground drawn as a single furrow running away to the horizon, as if the rod were the handle of a plough that has already cut one straight line. The muscle of the forearm is tensed and is the detailed part. The grip holds one thing only; the other hand is open and empty at the edge of the frame.

**Back**
- Band: **Implementation** · *Flexible ↔ Rigid*
- Tools:
  - Concrete asset ┘■ **Production**: structure, diligence. Weapon: *Coarseness* (overreach, stubbornness)
  - Abstract asset ◗● **Belief**: principles, enthusiasm. Weapon: *Mania* (ephemeral, relentlessness)
  - Concrete risk ┌□ **Completeness**: commitment, honesty. Weapon: *Defensive* (hiding, scrupulousness)
  - Abstract risk ◐○ **Momentum**: stability, resilience. Weapon: *Restlessness* (unadventurousness, toiling)
- Heard: the line stated calmly and kept against the actual violation.
- Screaming: "I've already done it"; force at the trigger; the wrong thing gripped harder.
- Too much / too little: steamrolling / irresolute, ineffectual.
- *Who do you know who commits to one course and carries it through without flattening anyone? What would they do here?*

## 6. Preservation — Certainty (teal)

**Front**
- Band: **6 · Preservation** · *Certainty*
- Emotion → verb: **Fear → take**
- Body: **the stomach and the lungs**
- Question: **What must be secured before exposure becomes loss?**
- Opposites: Unification · Implementation

**Drawing.** A low stone wall built of tested, fitted stones, with an open gate in the middle; through the gate, a storehouse with its door ajar and sacks inside, a visible count chalked on the doorpost. In the foreground a turtle, head out, walking toward the gate. The wall has a gate and the gate is open; that is the point of the image.

**Back**
- Band: **Preservation** · *Direct ↔ Indirect*
- Tools:
  - Concrete asset ┘■ **Budgeting**: prioritization, fortification. Weapon: *Corruption* (favoritism, redundancy)
  - Abstract asset ◗● **Salvaging**: repurposing, acceptance. Weapon: *Greed* (hoarding, liquidating)
  - Concrete risk ┌□ **Preparation**: utility, organization. Weapon: *Preemptive* (cowardice, perfectionism)
  - Abstract risk ◐○ **Calculation**: acumen, caution. Weapon: *Pessimism* (avoidant, hypochondria)
- Heard: the precaution taken once, with a criterion and a re-check time.
- Screaming: the freeze-out: a wall with no gate; checking that never ends.
- Too much / too little: controlling / careless, unprepared.
- *Who do you know who prepares for the worst and still leaves the gate open? What would they do here?*

---

## 7. Transformation — Excellence (purple)

**Front**
- Band: **7 · Transformation** · *Excellence*
- Emotion → verb: **Disgust → let go and realign**
- Body: **the breath of spirit**
- Question: **Do the process and the result still belong together, and what must be put down?**
- Opposites: Initiation · Deconstruction

**Drawing.** A mountain summit at night with a figure standing on it, seen small from behind, shoulders dropped, hands open. A boulder is rolling away down the slope behind the figure. Above, a few fixed stars, and from the figure's mouth a visible breath rising toward them, the one soft line in an otherwise hard-edged image. No fire; the breath is the light.

**Back**
- Band: **Transformation** · *Process ↔ Result*
- Tools:
  - Concrete asset ┘■ **Fulfillment**: presence, gratitude. Weapon: *Tantrum* (entitlement, naive)
  - Abstract asset ◗● **Character**: actualization, aspiration. Weapon: *Obsession* (nicheness, envy)
  - Concrete risk ┌□ **Attitude**: transcendence, reframing. Weapon: *Apathy* (rationalization, flippancy)
  - Abstract risk ◐○ **Idealism**: ethics, optimization. Weapon: *Extreme* (dogmatic, vulgar)
- Heard: the misaligned thing put down so process and result match again.
- Screaming: "this proves the whole relationship was a mistake"; a person made into contamination.
- Too much / too little: grandiose / shortsighted, stagnant.
- *Who do you know who can let go of what no longer fits without throwing out the whole thing? What would they do here?*

## Two extra cards worth printing

**The key card.** The data cube (■ ● ┘ ◗ ┌ ◐ □ ○) with the three rules: filled is asset, hollow is risk; square is concrete, round is abstract; a piece missing means interpreted. Below it the heptagon with all seven coloured sides and the seven dichotomy rings, so any value card can be placed on it.

**The method card.** Five lines: Pick your three favourites by what they do. Pick the three weapons you like least in others. The ones you dislike are the approach you have a taboo on; the two across the wheel are where you live. For each approach you are not seeing, name someone who is good at it. Ask what they would do.
